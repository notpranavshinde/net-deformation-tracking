Occlusion Lab

Dark patches were painted into real towing-tank footage, mostly while the net was deflecting fastest, and CoTracker3 was rerun on the edited video. Each covered knot is scored against where it really was, taken from the same run without the patch. The right camera is left untouched, as if only one lens were blocked.

It knows when it is guessing

The model flagged — of covered knot-frames as hidden, and almost never flagged an uncovered knot (—). So every inferred point can be marked as inferred in the output.

Short gaps are filled well

Up to a 4×4 block for 2 s, the median guess stays within — of the true knot, a few percent of the knot spacing, and after triangulation within about —.

Where it breaks

At 0.30 m/s a 3×3 block hidden for the rest of the clip drifts by a third of a knot spacing, and knot 88 at the corner of the 4×4 block came back on its neighbor and stayed there for about 2 s. The 20 px reprojection gate rejected — of that long-gap case.
